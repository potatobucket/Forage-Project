# Forage Spreadsheet Project
A practice assignment to provide an example of real-world data analysis by performing a spreadsheet analysis of content for the fictional company Social Buzz using [Google Sheets](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1iBjI0vhyq_33dK2-4P9mffDlE1OG0AR0eH6zvrvW5OQ/edit?usp=sharing) and [Google Slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1FYZ4f4EpJd7cyQJtTZfgD-ZRmPZHaSCDWDuYlalNR0o/edit?usp=sharing).

Skills and steps peformed:
 - Completed a simulation focused on advising a hypothetical social media client as a Data Analyst at Accenture
 - Cleaned, modelled and analyzed 7 datasets to uncover insights into content trends to inform strategic decisions
 - Prepared a PowerPoint deck and video presentation to communicate key insights for the client and internal stakeholders

to focus their efforts toward the top five categories of content by popularity.

## Findings
There were **sixteen** unique categories.

The top five categories by popularity turned out to be:
 - Animals - with 1,897 reactions alone
 - Science
 - Healthy Eating
 - Technology
 - Food
 
The most reactions recorded in a single month happened in **January.**
